Why it works

One rule. Every page.

A pencil and a blank page ask a lot of a three-year-old: where do I start, which way do I go, when am I done? Every page in these books answers all three first. Start at the black dot. Follow the path. Stop at the star. The rule never changes from book to book, so a child who has finished a maze already knows how a dot-to-dot begins.

Why a dot and a star

The dot is where the pencil goes down. The star is where it lifts off. Everything in between is the activity: a stroke of a letter, a road through a maze, a line from one number to the next.

Because the rule is the same everywhere, children spend their attention on the pencil, not on working out the page. Dot, the little yellow mascot, is on every cover to remind them.

Each book stands on its own, so start with whatever your child is ready for today. The chart further down sets the four side by side.

Good to know

Is there a Kindle edition?

No. These are write-in workbooks, and you can't trace a letter or draw through a maze on a Kindle screen, so they come as paperbacks only.

What size are the books?

All four are 8.5 × 11 inches, the size of a sheet of printer paper, so there is room for big, wobbly first lines. They run from 80 to 130 pages.

Are the answers included?

Yes, in both maze books: the answers are at the back. In the letter book and the dot-to-dot book, the finished letter or picture is the answer.

Where can I buy them?

On Amazon in the US, UK, Germany, Canada and Australia. The links under each book go straight to it.
